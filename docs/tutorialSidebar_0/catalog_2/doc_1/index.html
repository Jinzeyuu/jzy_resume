01 Audiovisual emotion recognition based on bi-layer LSTM and multi-head attention mechanism on RAVDESS dataset​

（1）论文级别​

  • SCI3区非开源期刊---第一作者

（2）摘要​

Recognizing and classifying emotions is important. It is key in medicine, transportation, and artificial intelligence. This paper aims to address the problem. The problem is poor emotion classification due to a bad fusion of many modes. It proposes an audio–visual emotion recognition method. It is based on bilayer LSTM and multi-head attention on the RAVDESS dataset. It considers the fusion of voice and facial expression features. The network learns MFCC and facial features. It uses a convolutional layer for MFCC features and a double-layer LSTM for facial features. Then, it fuses the features of both modalities using a multi-head attention module. Finally, it convolves, pools, and splices the learned features for emotion recognition. In our experiments, the accuracy on the public dataset RAVDESS is 82.42%.We got this result using 5-fold cross-validation. Comparison with other methods shows that the method improves audio–visual emotion recognition.

02 Monitoring of Smoking Behavior in Construction Sites of The Power System​

（1）论文级别​

  • EI会议论文---第一作者

（2）摘要​

The monitoring of no-smoking behavior in the construction sites of the power system is crucial in ensuring the normal operation of the power system. For the traditional smoke detectors with low accuracy and easy false detection, etc. Based on the principle of target detection and the training of the cigarette dataset, a yolov5-based smoking behavior monitoring and identification method is proposed. The adopted dataset includes a total of 7027 pictures of smokers and fire sources, divided into a training set of 5741, a test set of 644, and a validation set of 706, and the model is trained on the dataset by yolov5. The training results show that: the recognition accuracy of this paper's method for no-smoking behavior monitoring is about 0.82, the average accuracy is 0.803, the inference speed is up to 8ms, and the model size is 13.7MB. The method can realize the recognition and framing of cigarettes as well as fires in power system construction sites with a high recognition rate, which effectively improves the management efficiency of power system construction sites.

04 Fire and Smoke Detection of Power System Transmission and Distribution Equipment​

（1）论文级别​

  • EI会议论文---第三作者

（2）摘要​

Fire in power system is easy to cause heavy casualties and a large number of property losses. Because of the complex and changeable environment, it is important to accurate early fire detection in current research hotspot issues. Aiming at the fire safety detection of power system transmission, transformation and distribution equipment, a smoke and fire target detection method based on YOLOV5 model is proposed according to the questions of small target size, which is easy to be confused with the actual situation. According to the characteristics of smoke and fire movement changes, the network is improved, and a detection layer is added to the original detection layer, so that the feature map can continue to be up-sampled and the detection accuracy can be improved.

05 A Remote End-to-end Real-time Heart Rate Detection Method Based on rPPG in Driving Scenarios​

（1）论文级别​

  • EI会议论文---第四作者

（2）摘要​

Driver heart rate (HR) detection is a significant research topic, and real-time understanding of the driver's physiological state can significantly improve driving safety. Camera-based remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) technology can be an alternative to inconvenient wearable devices for noncontact HR detection. However, although rPPG technology has been extensively researched, its application to driving scenarios requires overcoming various disturbances in outdoor conditions, particularly the effects of light variations. To apply rPPG technology to driving scenarios, we propose two improvements. First, we utilize an exposure correction model to preprocess the input image to minimize the effect of illumination variations on the robustness of the prediction. Second, we propose an end-to-end HR detection network based on the improvement of Rhythmnet, which makes it possible to achieve real-time HR detection without the need to perform additional image processing steps. Compared with existing HR detection methods for driving scenarios, our method achieved significant improvements, and experiments on the MR-NIRP Car public dataset achieved the best or near-best results.
